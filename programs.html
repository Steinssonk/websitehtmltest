Flight Programs

Ascend through the ranks and unlock new perks along your journey.

Line Flying

Second Officer

Begin your line-flying career. Perfect for junior aviators looking to build hours and get accustomed to our standard operational procedures.

  • Access to regional fleet aircraft
  • Short-haul route clearance
  • Standard company benefits
Advanced Operations

First Officer

Take on more responsibility as an experienced pilot. This program opens doors to larger airframes and lucrative medium-haul assignments.

  • Access to medium-haul fleet
  • Priority route scheduling
  • Increased hourly compensation
Command Path

Captain

Command the skies. Reserved for dedicated aviators, this role offers the highest level of autonomy, respect, and long-haul capabilities.

  • Heavy and long-haul aircraft clearance
  • Unrestricted route access
  • Command-level dispatch priority
