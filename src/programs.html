Development programs

Specialized training.
Rapid advancement.

Two initiatives built to fast-track pilots who don't yet hold the license they need.

Jet Engine License Program
The Udyan Leaf

Focused on players who don't yet hold a Jet Engine License. This program grants pilots a strong choice of aircraft to start flying jets sooner.

  • Choice of any jet-engine aircraft in our fleet.
  • Get 22.5k WP to cover license fees, upon acceptance.
Apply for The Udyan Leaf
Supersonic Transport License
The Udyan Stem

Focused on pilots who don't yet hold an SST license. This program offers major benefits and a choice of starter supersonic aircraft.

  • Choice between the Concorde and Boom Overture 16'.
  • Get 100k WP to cover license fees, upon acceptance.
